# Riyadh–Colombo Route Launch Simulator

## Project Overview

This project evaluates whether a hypothetical Saudi low-cost airline should launch a direct service between Riyadh (RUH) and Colombo (CMB).

The analysis will combine market sizing, passenger segmentation, competitive assessment and route-level financial modelling to determine whether the proposed route could be commercially viable.

## Business Question

> Should a Saudi low-cost carrier launch Riyadh–Colombo, at what frequency, and under what commercial conditions?

## Analysis Objectives

1. Estimate the addressable passenger demand between Saudi Arabia and Sri Lanka.
2. Identify the main customer segments and travel purposes.
3. Assess direct and indirect competition.
4. Develop realistic fare, load-factor and ancillary-revenue assumptions.
5. Estimate flight-level and annual route economics.
6. Calculate the route’s break-even load factor.
7. Test conservative, base and upside scenarios.
8. Identify the main commercial and operational risks.
9. Recommend whether the airline should launch, test seasonally or reject the route.

## Proposed Operating Model

The initial model will test:

- Airbus A320-family aircraft
- Riyadh as the origin base
- Colombo as the destination
- Low-cost carrier operating model
- Multiple weekly-frequency options
- Economy-class passenger revenue
- Ancillary revenue from baggage, seats, meals and priority services

## Project Outputs

- Market-demand assessment
- Competitor and schedule analysis
- Route financial model
- Break-even analysis
- Scenario and sensitivity testing
- Commercial risk assessment
- Final route-launch recommendation
